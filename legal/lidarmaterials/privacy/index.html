Wallwise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Summary

Wallwise does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your scans, photos and projects stay on your device.

Camera and LiDAR

The camera and the LiDAR scanner are used only while you scan a wall, a floor, a room or a patch of ground. The depth data and the 3D surface are processed on your device to work out areas and volumes. One photo of the scanned surface is kept with the project so you can see what was measured. Nothing is uploaded.

Your projects

Projects, measurements and shopping lists are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They may be included in your own iCloud or device backups, which Apple handles under its privacy policy. When you share a list, you choose where it goes.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your name, email address or payment details.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete everything the app stores with Settings → Delete All Projects, or by deleting the app. For any question write to ppejic89@gmail.com.

Changes and contact
