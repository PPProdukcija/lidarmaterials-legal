Which devices can scan

Scanning needs the LiDAR scanner: iPhone 12 Pro and later Pro models, and iPad Pro (2020 and later). On other devices, enter the sizes by hand — the shopping list works the same.

Getting a good scan

Turn on the light. Stand two to four metres from the wall, point the dot at it and tap Start. Sweep slowly from corner to corner, then tilt down to the floor and up to the ceiling so the height locks. The live map shows what is still missing.

Windows, doors and furniture

Openings are found and left out on their own; switch one back on to cover it anyway. Wall or floor hidden behind furniture is still counted.

Subscriptions

Restore Purchases is in the app's Settings. To cancel, open your Apple Account subscriptions.

Your data

Projects stay on your device. To delete them all, open Settings → Delete All Projects. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
